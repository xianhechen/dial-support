02 / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

Last updated: October 28, 2026

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

dial is developed by Michael Chen. Your chemical inventory, mix dates, processed-roll counts, presets, timer settings and active-run checkpoint are stored on your device. No account is required. dial does not automatically send this information to the developer and has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs.

ALARMS & SYSTEM FEATURES

If you allow alarms, dial shares the timing and step information needed by Apple's alarm and Live Activity features with the operating system. Step names and countdowns can appear on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. You can manage alarm permission in Settings. dial has no app-managed cloud sync; Apple may include local app data in device backups according to your system settings.

IF YOU CONTACT SUPPORT

Email is optional. If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address and the message or attachments you send through your email provider. dial does not automatically attach your chemical inventory or presets. Support correspondence is used to answer your request and troubleshoot the app, and kept only as needed for those purposes or applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the same address.

YOUR CHOICES & DELETION

You can edit or delete chemicals and presets in the app. They are separate records, so deleting a chemical does not delete saved presets. Cancel an active run to end its alarms. Deleting the app removes its local app data; device backups are managed separately in Apple's settings. The developer cannot access or delete your on-device records remotely.

PUBLIC WEBSITE

If you visit a dial support or privacy page on GitHub Pages, your browser sends a web request to GitHub, which handles connection information under its own privacy statement. These pages use no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies added by us.

CHANGES & CONTACT

This policy may be updated when dial changes. The public policy shows the latest revision date. For privacy questions or support, contact folio.coffee.journal+dial@gmail.com.
