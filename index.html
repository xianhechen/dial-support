REPORTING A PROBLEM

Please include your app version, device model, iOS version and what happened. For timer issues, include the step and whether dial was open, in the background or locked. Only share screenshots or records you want us to see.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST ROLL

Check step times against your film and chemical instructions. Run a short process without film to check alarms on your device. Allow alarms for dial in Settings to use background alerts.

YOUR DATA

Your inventory and presets are stored on your device. No account is required. For details about local data, optional support email and this website, read our Privacy Policy.
